# Art approvals (Ascension 0.2.0)

Plan 2026-10-07-kestrel-depth, Task 30. A sign-off list for **you**: every visual below was made or changed by an agent
and judged only on software-rendered captures on a private display. None of it has been approved by a person. Mark each
row Pass or Fail (with a note) before the itch upload (`docs/release/itch-checklist.md`). A Fail is a follow-up task,
not a blocker for this list.

## How to look

- **In game.** `./run-3d.sh`. For anything on the alien side, first open Settings on the title and set Fog of war to
  Off (it applies at the next match), then New operation, Kestrel Reach, Deploy forces. The alien base is east of the
  river; its Core Node stands near tile (37, 8). `M` opens the strategic map, the arrow keys pan.
- **Review captures.** The `task-*.png` files named below are the agents' review images in
  `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-07-kestrel-depth/` (and, for the building sheet, `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-06-kestrel-next-level/`).
  That folder is local scratch, ignored by git, so the files exist only on this machine. They are software GL (llvmpipe)
  frames: colours, shadows and the shroud may look different on the RTX 3050.
- **Sprite files.** Building sprites are `godot3d/assets/world/sprites/<name>.png` (256 x 256), generated by
  `tools/ui/build_building_variants.py`. Any image viewer shows them; nothing needs the game.

## 3. Not art, but needs your eyes or ears too

- Sound: no person has heard any of the 26 effects. `python3 tools/audio/listen_pass.py --notes listen-notes.txt` from a
  terminal (it asks first and plays one sound at a time).
- Music: there is none. The game decides menu, calm, combat, victory and defeat states (`godot3d/scripts/audio/music_director.gd`)
  but plays nothing until tracks are added to its `TRACKS` table.
- GPU look: `tools/perf/gpu_frametime.sh --allow-user-display` on the RTX 3050; while it runs, look at the fog shroud,
  the shadows and the shield ring.
