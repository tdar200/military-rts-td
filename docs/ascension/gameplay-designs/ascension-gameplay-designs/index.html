IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

Build now, or add generation first?

  1. Placement validity is authoritative and rechecked at command execution; preview validity is advisory.
  2. Reject overlap with solid buildings, out-of-map cells and hostile footprints. Friendly units receive an eviction order; construction waits for a clear site.
  3. Construction progresses only with staff, work capacity and delivered materials. Power shortage appears as an explicit pause or throttle reason.
  4. Maintain separate available, reserved and consumed ledgers. Reservation must be atomic across rapid repeated placements.
  5. Territory follows completed anchors; losing an anchor pauses new placements, not demolition of surviving facilities.
  6. Illustrative proposed costs and incremental consumption are design changes, not a description of the current runtime.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

Hold the ridge or push through the valley?

  1. Proposed damage model: effective armour = clamp(armour − penetration, 0, 0.8); damage = max(1, base_damage × (1 − effective_armour)).
  2. Controlled AT example: 900 HP target, 81 HP per hit, 3 s cycle, first shot at t=0. Twelve hits kill at t=33 s. Travel time and movement add separately.
  3. No random accuracy in the baseline test model. Cover modifies exposed hit area through a documented multiplier; aircraft eligibility is a target-class rule.
  4. Range test uses squared distance, inclusive boundary. LOS, target class and ammunition are checked again when a shot is emitted.
  5. Hold means never pursue beyond assigned position radius. Attack-move can engage along the route; explicit target orders expire on target death.
  6. Formation slots are a soft objective. Narrow corridors temporarily use a column; regroup beyond the obstruction. Never force units through walls.
  7. Older units keep useful roles through low cost, low energy demand and specialized counter technology, not universal era damage scaling.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

Repair the route or retreat while covered?

  1. Supply route overlays show actual traversable connectivity, not straight-line distance alone.
  2. Early slice uses material-funded local ammunition distribution. Fuel, spare parts and routed convoys in this board are future design extensions where shown.
  3. No ammunition permits movement and retreat but no weapon discharge. Aircraft retain a separately visible reserve-return rule in the full design.
  4. Each shipment owns a unique reservation. Destruction moves contents to a wreck ledger or documented loss, never to both destination and source.
  5. Automatic routing minimizes travel time plus exposed-threat cost; player waypoints override with a visible risk notice.
  6. Economy buffer warning predicts remaining supply under current consumption, and labels the forecast as an estimate.
  7. Repair work consumes a separate parts/material budget and cannot create ammo. Depots cannot distribute more than they hold.

Counter-battery strike or defence in depth?

  1. Current early-wave baseline: first arrival at 90 s; later arrivals at 180 s intervals; doctrine announced 45 s before arrival.
  2. Composition shown here is illustrative. Spawn counts must be funded by the invasion budget plus operational relay contributions.
  3. AI planner scores infrastructure value, exposure, route cost and expected losses. Tactical controller chooses legal targets with hysteresis to avoid target thrashing.
  4. Doctrine observes a rolling history; it does not read queued human orders. Apply capped adaptation so all unit families remain viable.
  5. Spawn positions are validated outside occupied solid cells. A blocked edge selects another legal entry, never deletes the wave silently.
  6. Give each assault a progress watchdog. If no damage or advance occurs, change route, breach, or regroup; record the decision for QA.
  7. The player can see the map; uncertainty concerns future intent, production capacity and unstudied weapons.

Commit the factory, or postpone the era transition?

  1. Research graph is a directed acyclic graph; every prerequisite, unit unlock and facility ID resolves before shipping.
  2. Separate theory work, experimental evidence, prototype work and deployment eligibility; a single points bar cannot substitute for all four.
  3. Proposed retrofit: 120 materials + 20 s downtime buys +25% compatible work rate and +30 kW demand. Pay per facility; this is a design value, not current balance.
  4. Example factory: 12 work/s, 360 work per tank. Baseline 30 s; retrofitted 15 work/s gives 24 s with full supply. At 50% served capacity, 48 s.
  5. Prototype reservation is exclusive and recorded in saves. Another laboratory cannot clone the same prototype progress or reserve the same bay.
  6. Era UI previews new capabilities and continuing constraints before commit. No technology is completed by merely entering an era.
  7. Destruction of infrastructure pauses requirements-dependent work; established knowledge remains unless a explicitly designed scenario says otherwise.

Finish the Core, or divert to save headquarters?

  1. Core health and terminal state are authoritative. Clients cannot award victory from a local animation.
  2. The simultaneous-destruction outcome is a proposed explicit rule requiring implementation; do not infer it from the current ordering of damage events.
  3. Every weapon class that can legally damage structures can finish the Core. A projectile already emitted may finish it after its attacker dies.
  4. Defeat debrief shows what failed, the last supply interruption and the timing of production pauses; avoid a bare “You lose” screen.
  5. A resource deadlock prompts recovery options before declaring defeat. Losing all combat units alone is not defeat while production remains possible.
  6. Camera keeps the Core visible during the final assault but never steals control before terminal resolution.
  7. Cosmic campaign reveal is narrative progression, not proof that later layers are currently playable.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

How much authority should a planetary governor have?

  1. Full future design: tactical entities aggregate into cohorts with count, equipment, HP distribution, ammunition and orders.
  2. Use a single owner for each simulation domain. Change ownership transactionally; do not run tactical and aggregate production on the same assets.
  3. Zoom is presentation only. Time speed is explicit, bounded by active engagements, and applied consistently to travel, research and resource flows.
  4. Planetary logistics respects launch mass, transfer windows, travel time and receiving capacity. A space elevator raises throughput, not free material.
  5. Interstellar orders execute on delivery under the chosen physical model. Standing local orders prevent the entire civilization waiting for the player.
  6. No FTL logistics before the explicitly speculative branch. Antimatter is manufactured energy storage, not net energy creation.
  7. Army groups retain composition vulnerabilities: a system with many transports and no escorts is not equivalent to an equally expensive combat fleet.

  • A destroyed destination reroutes or holds cargo under policy.
  • Governor exceptions surface before an irreversible policy violation.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

Expand the network or preserve a survivable reserve?

  1. Era 05 interstellar: propulsion and autonomous probes; Era 06 stellar: swarms, lifting and stellar industry; Era 07 post-biological: optional digital and hybrid societies.
  2. Era 08 spacetime: explicitly speculative gateways and gravity research; Era 09 galactic: sector policies and distributed warfare; Era 10 cosmic: survival under causal and thermodynamic limits.
  3. Dyson swarms are orbiting collectors; no unsupported rigid sphere. Stellar engines use momentum transfer. Black-hole energy extraction has an explicit source of mass or spin.
  4. Relativistic strike: large preparation cost, visible launch infrastructure and long lead time; counter with dispersal, interception and relocation. No instant uncounterable kill button.
  5. Gateways impose mass/energy throughput and stabilization costs. Capturing a gateway does not grant unlimited instantaneous movement.
  6. Digital minds require compute, power and backup consistency. Biological populations require habitats, ecology and care; neither path is mandatory or a pure stat upgrade.
  7. Scenario physics choices are disclosed before campaign start and stored in the save. Never silently introduce faster-than-light solutions in a hard-science campaign.
EXPLORE STATES / DESIGN PREVIEW

SECTOR / AUTONOMOUS

Input: Set fleet and production policy

Result: Governors operate inside energy, matter and compute budgets. A local emergency spends only its reserved contingency allocation.

DISRUPTION / ISOLATED

Input: A gateway or star-power hub is lost

Result: Sever the route, expose the affected civilian reserve and execute fallback policy. Redundant sectors retain useful agency.

COSMIC / SURVIVAL

Input: Complete the resilience contract

Result: Victory evaluates retained minds, information, redundancy and sustainable power—not only destroyed enemy headquarters.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKS
  • Each exotic weapon has a cost, warning, counter and aftermath.
  • Sector automation cannot spend the same reserve twice.
  • No energy-positive antimatter production loop exists.
  • Victory checks resilience across disruption cycles, not a momentary stockpile spike.
