# Science boundary

Era dates are narrative horizons, never forecasts. Science categories describe the **complete named capability**, not whether one supporting equation is real. A technology inspired by general relativity is not thereby an achievable engineering project.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Established | Relevant capability is demonstrated; battlefield implementation remains a game abstraction. |
| Experimental | Components or experiments exist; required integrated performance is unproven. |
| Theoretically plausible | No new physical law is assumed, but feasibility and scalable engineering are unproved. |
| Highly speculative | Depends on major unresolved scientific or engineering assumptions. |
| Unknown physics required | Gameplay requires a capability for which there is no established physical mechanism. |

“Engineering extrapolation” corresponds chiefly to Experimental and Theoretically plausible. The last label is the explicit fictional-mechanics boundary, not a prediction that further funding must eventually make the idea work. No era is inherently more scientifically credible than another: late stellar collectors may need fewer new assumptions than early consciousness uploading.

## Mandatory corrections to the progression fantasy

**Antimatter stores previously supplied energy.** Its annihilation releases energy, but manufactured antimatter is not a primary source of net energy. Production, capture, containment and conversion introduce losses. Its gameplay value is transportable energy density and propulsion, with expensive production and containment. [CERN overview](https://home.cern/science/physics/antimatter/) and [CERN explanation](https://cds.cern.ch/record/1965944/files/CERN-Brochure-2014-009-Eng.pdf).

**Fusion is an industrial chain.** Fuel breeding, neutron-resistant materials and heat removal belong in the unlock path; plasma performance alone does not commission a power station. Deuterium–tritium is the initial route. Helium-3 is an optional demanding later route, not a miracle fuel required for all fusion. [ITER engineering requirements](https://www.iter.org/fusion-energy/making-it-work) and [breeding programme](https://www.iter.org/machine/supporting-systems/tritium-breeding).

**Wormholes are hypothetical.** Mathematical solutions do not establish that traversable, stable or constructible wormholes exist. Macroscopic negative-energy support and stability remain assumptions, not a mined resource proven by physics. The game may name a fictional resource “exotic stress capacity” only in the speculative ruleset. [NASA discussion](https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/attic/cosmicopia.gsfc.nasa.gov/qa_sp_sl.html).

**Computation has thermodynamic and engineering costs.** Reversible logic can reduce particular dissipation costs; it does not mean free computing, perfect error correction or zero cooling. The design prices clock speed, redundancy and heat separately. [Bennett, Notes on Landauer’s principle](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0210005).

**Visible does not mean reachable.** Cosmic expansion and communication horizons constrain what a civilization can influence. Millions of years do not automatically permit control of the observable universe. The grounded ending protects a causally reachable domain; universal control is a labelled fictional campaign extension. [NASA cosmology explanations](https://imagine.gsfc.nasa.gov/ask_astro/cosmology.html).

These references support scientific boundaries, not the invented technology dates, costs, balance numbers, alien history or campaign events. Detailed late-era concepts remain design hypotheses requiring specialist review before educational claims are published.

## Rulesets

**Causal frontier (default):** no faster-than-light transport or communication, no extractable vacuum-energy budget, no artificial-universe victory requirement. Remote colonies operate autonomously; strategic play compresses time but respects travel and signal schedules. Grounded alternatives carry the player through all ten thematic eras.

**Speculative frontier (opt-in):** explicitly adds a finite list of fictional postulates: stable artificial traversable gates, engineered exotic stress, and optional vacuum/cosmogenesis scenarios. Both sides obey the same rules. Unlock screens restate the physical assumption and its gameplay constraints. No infinite energy, instantaneous global response or retroactive actions.

Each Highly speculative or Unknown physics required catalogue entry includes an inspiration, required assumption, physics status and understandable gameplay effect. Universal-control, vacuum-state and artificial-universe projects are opt-in alternate endings. Entanglement never provides faster-than-light communication in the causal ruleset.

## Extreme capabilities and enforceable limits

| Capability | Inspiration and missing assumption | Game implementation and counter |
|---|---|---|
| Dyson swarm | Orbital collectors; enormous autonomous manufacturing remains unproved | Many independent collectors; exposed transmission and radiator capacity; distributed repair. No rigid shell assumed. |
| Stellar engines | Momentum conservation, asymmetric radiation or mass ejection; sustained stellar engineering unproved | Relocate routes over long strategic intervals; commits mass flow and exposes collectors; enemy can deny departure infrastructure. |
| Star lifting | Magnetized plasma and stellar winds; controlled extraction at useful scale unproved | Finite stellar composition, separation plants and energy investment; extraction changes stellar evolution budget. |
| Black-hole plant | Accretion and rotating-hole energy extraction; stable industrial control unproved | Feedstock, alignment, radiators and finite spin reserve; never perpetual motion. |
| Artificial black holes | Relativity and proposed high-energy concentration; production and handling unknown | Optional experiment project; failed containment loses the test facility, not an arbitrary entire campaign. |
| Digital minds | Neuroscience and computation; conscious functional emulation unproved | Optional population substrate with compute, memory, backup and cooling costs; biological route stays competitive. |
| Wormhole network | Traversable spacetime solutions; stable exotic support unestablished | Paired endpoints must first be delivered conventionally; finite mass and power throughput; long restart and endpoint blockade. |
| Metric displacement | General relativity; controllable metric engineering unestablished | Optional prepared-lane relocation; expensive anchors, advance warning, no arrival inside occupied hulls. |
| Matter transmission | Information and fabrication; arbitrary exact transfer not demonstrated | Fictional scanner/destructive-source rule plus destination feedstock; no duplicate mass, no free cargo. |
| Vacuum engineering | Quantum field vacuum structure; useful controllable transitions unknown | Optional bounded experimental scenario zone; costs stellar power and suspends adjacent gates. Never presented as proven unlimited energy. |
| Artificial universe | Speculative cosmogenesis; laboratory creation, access and survivability unknown | Optional terminal project with explicit fictional postulates; cannot extract resources from an unmodelled universe. |
| Cosmic information network | Relays under relativity, or fictional gates | Time-stamped regional state in causal mode; faster relay only through built speculative links. |
