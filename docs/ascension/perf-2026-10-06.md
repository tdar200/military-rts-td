# Kestrel perf after the next-level plan, 2026-10-06

Branch `kestrel-next-level`, after Tasks 1 to 9 (icons, opening, objectives, sound, fog, minimap, building art, lean).
Software only: every number below is llvmpipe on a private Xvfb (:94), Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility, 1920x1080, Dummy audio
driver. The GPU (RTX 3050) was not used, so the idle frame budget of the 2026-10-05 note (10 ms p50 uncapped on the GPU)
is **unmeasured**. The machine was heavily loaded by unrelated work (load average about 40 on 12 cores), so fps and
process times are not meaningful; draw calls and primitives are deterministic and are the gate.

## Method

- `EARTHFRONT_FOG=0 python3 tools/qa/world_capture.py <dir> --software --display :94` for the fog-off frames (the
  scoreboard state), and the same without the variable for the default fogged frames. Each shot's JSON records the
  engine's draw calls and primitives for that frame (HUD hidden, Kestrel idle about 8 s after deploy).
- Budget (plan Global Constraints, from the 2026-10-05 note): at most +120 draw calls and +150k primitives over
  `tools/ui/world_baseline.json` `_perf` (home 574 / 138,388; max 1,446 / 223,498, the same software method).

- Fog on draws fewer calls where the enemy would be: hidden entities have no nodes. The fog itself is a uniform branch in
  shaders already drawn (no new shader or pass), so the home frame is unchanged by it.
- The +18.7k primitives at home are the Task 2 prebuilt base (Power Plant, Refinery, Barracks, Harvester) and earlier
  world art; they were already present at the start of this plan's later tasks (Task 2 measured the same 35 / 157,082).
- The draw call counts sit far under the `_perf` baseline; Tasks 7 to 9 reported the same relation (home -539). Why the
  baseline frame drew so many more calls was not investigated here; the comparison is the plan's fixed reference.
- Task 9's building art and lean did not change any idle shot (the same numbers as Tasks 7 and 8, apart from an effect
  card in flight on one enemy shot in Task 9's own capture).

## Live session (software, loaded machine)

The Task 10 scripted playthrough (real bridge and client, Xvfb, Dummy audio) ran Godot at 1 to 2 fps while the bridge
kept about 22 ticks per second (it reached tick 7288 in 331 s of wall time). That is the machine and llvmpipe, not a
measurement of the game; it does mean combat sounds and effects were sampled at a few polls per second only.
